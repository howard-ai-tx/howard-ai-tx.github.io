The security layer inside every Howard.

What It Is

One system, watching everything.

Arken runs continuously in the background of every Howard. It decides who has access, checks what's allowed to leave the building, and keeps an eye on the system's own health — all without a cloud connection.

Built in, not bolted on

Every Howard ships with Arken pre-installed.

Nothing sent off-site

Arken makes its decisions on the hardware in front of you.

How It Works

Four areas. One system.

Access

Manages who's allowed to do what, and grows more capable as trust builds.

Defense

Reviews requests before they reach anywhere else and flags anything unusual.

Awareness

Watches its own health and keeps a readable record of what it stores.

Presence

Handles voice and smart home connections without leaving the building.
