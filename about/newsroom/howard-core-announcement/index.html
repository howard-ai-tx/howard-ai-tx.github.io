HowardAI announces Howard Core, a locally hosted AI assistant for homes and small businesses.

HowardAI today announced Howard Core, the company's launch product and first locally hosted AI assistant for homes and small businesses. It runs on dedicated hardware in the customer's home or office, executing tasks rather than just answering questions.

Howard books flights, manages calendars, handles email, and builds new skills over time — connected through whichever messaging platform the customer already uses. Data stays local by default; an optional cloud connection for non-sensitive queries is opt-in, not required.

Setup is self-install, no technician required. Howard Core is not yet available for purchase; pre-orders open in the coming months.

Made because AI shouldn't run your life. It should take care of the things that do.
