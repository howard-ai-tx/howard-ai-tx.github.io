The product

What exactly does Howard do?

Executes tasks — booking travel, calendars, email, admin work — and builds new skills over time. You reach it through whatever messaging app you already use.

Is Howard a chatbot?

Technically it's built on the same models. The difference is it takes action — books the flight, doesn't just tell you how.

What hardware does Howard run on?

A self-contained unit that sits in your home or office. Everything needed is in the box.

How do I interact with Howard?

Through your preferred messaging app, configured during setup. No new interface to learn.

Does Howard work if my internet goes down?

Yes — core processing is local. Tasks needing outside services (booking, sending) need a connection; the assistant itself doesn't.

Privacy & data

Where does my data go?

Nowhere, by default. Sensitive data stays on your hardware. Non-sensitive queries can optionally route to the cloud — you set what counts as sensitive.

Is my data used to train AI models?

No. Howard learns your preferences locally; the underlying models never see your data.

How is Howard different from cloud AI products on privacy?

Cloud AI promises privacy in a policy. Howard makes it structural — there's no server to leak from.

What does the monthly subscription cover?

Updates, security monitoring, support, and a same-day replacement warranty.
